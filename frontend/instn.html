How to Register & Pay via PSG EMS

Step-by-step procedure to complete your verification and event registration on the official portal.

Important: Enter the exact same 10-digit mobile number and email ID you used for your SRiSHTi account so your payment automatically syncs.
1

Mobile Number & OTP Verification

Open the EMS portal. Enter your 10-digit mobile number (+91) in the Contact Information section.

  • Click the blue Verify button below the input.
  • Enter the OTP received on your mobile phone to complete phone verification.
2

Email Address & OTP Verification

Once your phone is verified, the Email field unlocks.

  • Enter your registered email address.
  • Click Verify and enter the OTP received in your inbox.
3

Upload Photo & Personal Details

Fill out the profile details required by the institution:

  • Photo: Upload your passport-size photo (JPG, PNG, WEBP — max 512KB).
  • Prefix & Name: Choose prefix (Mr./Ms./Dr.) and enter your full name.
  • Gender: Select your gender from the dropdown.
4

Professional Details & Participant Type Crucial

Under Professional Information > Professional Details, select the Participant Type carefully from the search dropdown:

Dropdown on the PSG EMS portal • (Tap to view full)

1. For General Registration (Events, Paper & Flagships)

Select the option corresponding to your college:

  • PSG Tech Students: Select PSG CT - Student
  • PSG iTech Students: Select PSGiTech - Student
  • All Other College Students: Select Student

2. For Workshops

Type or scroll in the dropdown to choose your specific workshop name directly:

  • Example: Workshop on From Pixels to Intelligence
  • Example: Workshop on Powering Future Mobility
  • Or your respective registered workshop title.

3. Institute Name & Pincode

  • Institute / Organization Name: Enter your college name (e.g. PSGCT or your college name).
  • Pincode: Enter the college postal pincode (e.g. 641030).
⚠️ Caution: Selecting an incorrect participant type will prevent your payment from linking with your SRiSHTi account or workshop seat.
5

Review & Complete Payment

Click Proceed to Review, double-check your entered information, and complete the transaction on the payment gateway (UPI, Net Banking, or Cards).

  • Save a screenshot or download the payment receipt containing the transaction ID.
6

Sync with your SRiSHTi Profile

After completing payment on EMS, return to the SRiSHTi website:

  • Log in with your SRiSHTi ID and mobile number.
  • Visit your Profile page.
  • Click Update Details (or Sync) to pull your payment status from EMS. Your fee status will change to Paid.

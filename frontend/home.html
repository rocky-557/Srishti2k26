THE INITIATIVE

IEEE SC 12951 at PSG College of Technology was initiated in 1977. It was established to engage engineering visionaries in research and pioneer high-impact ideas.

PSG Tech has consistently set benchmark standards in engineering excellence and is among the first premier institutions in the nation to host an active IEEE Student Branch. The chapter actively organizes top-tier national technical symposiums year after year, driving the largest technical community at PSG Tech.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do I need prior technical knowledge for events and workshops?+
Not at all. Bring your enthusiasm and drive — all foundational skills and guidance will be provided!
Can we form inter-college teams?+
Yes, absolutely! Teams can consist of members across different engineering colleges and departments.
What are the general registration fees?+
General registration is ₹250 for PSG Tech students and ₹300 for external delegates.
Do workshops require a separate registration pass?+
Yes. Each workshop has dedicated enrollment, and you can register directly without general entry tickets.

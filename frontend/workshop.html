    DESCRIPTION

    Bot Blitz is a two-round robotics competition that tests speed, precision, and control. Teams first navigate their robots through an obstacle course, and the top performers advance to a robot bowling challenge using the same robot. The event evaluates navigation, handling, strategy, and accuracy in a competitive environment.

    EVENT: BOT BLITZ
    CATEGORY: BOT EVENT
    VENUE: MAKERSPACE, G 609 (DAY 1)

    ROUND 1

    SPEED TRAILS
    Race the Course. Master Every Obstacle.

    Round 1 is a speed and navigation challenge where teams guide their robots through an obstacle-filled arena featuring ramps, slaloms, bends, bridges, target touch zones, and precision stops. The objective is to complete the course in the shortest possible time while avoiding penalties for touching obstacles, crossing boundaries, or missing checkpoints. The round tests robot handling, speed, precision, and control.

    ROUND 2

    STRIKE ZONE
    Aim with precision. Strike with confidence.

    Round 2 is a robot bowling challenge where teams use the same robot from round 1 to push a bowling ball and knock down as many pins as possible. Each team is given multiple frames with limited attempts, making every move count. Success depends on precision, control, strategy, and accurate ball placement. The team with the highest bowling score is declared the winner, while ties are resolved based on a sudden-death frame.
